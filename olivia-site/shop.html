Made to order

Each piece is built when you order it, finished by hand in your choice of New Orleans Patina, Provincial, or Special Walnut, and sealed with General Finishes topcoat.

We ship nationwide via white-glove delivery. Larger items can be picked up in Natchitoches or delivered white-glove at approximately $1.85 per mile from the shop.
